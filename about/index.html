About

Cask & Cup is a small independent journal about coffee and American whiskey. It is published independently and written in Wisconsin.

The aim is practical: how to read a bourbon label, how to brew a repeatable pour-over at home, how coffee and bourbon actually sit together, and what phrases like “barrel-aged” mean when they show up on a bag of beans. The site is not a liquor store, a cafe, or a collecting tip sheet for allocated bottles.

Editorial approach

Articles here are written as guides, not as personal tasting diaries. They do not invent statistics, invent quotations, or pretend a bottle was poured in this kitchen unless that is actually the case. Where prices are mentioned, they are typical ranges that vary by state, store, and season — including in Wisconsin.

Alcohol-related writing is for readers who are 21 or older in the United States. Please drink slowly and do not drive.

How the site is supported

Cask & Cup is meant, over time, to be supported by display advertising (starting with Google AdSense, if and when the site is approved) and by affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link and buy something, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That relationship will not be used as a reason to invent praise for a product. A site-wide disclosure appears on every page, and the Privacy Policy explains ads, cookies, and affiliates in more detail.

Until ads are approved, the pages are kept free of third-party ad scripts.

What you will not find

This is not a place to buy alcohol, and it is not legal, medical, or financial advice. It also does not publish street addresses or phone numbers for the publisher. If you need to get in touch, use the Contact page.
