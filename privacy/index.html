Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how Cask & Cup (“the site,” “we”) handles information. The site is published independently in Wisconsin. It is a static website: we do not run a user account system, and we do not ask you to create a login.

If we make a material change to this policy, we will update this page and change the effective date below.

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Information we may have

This site does not require you to submit personal information to read articles. If you email us through the address on the Contact page, we will have whatever you include in that message (typically your email address and the content of the message) so we can reply. We use that information only to respond, unless you ask us to do something else or we are required by law to keep it.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children. Alcohol-related content on this site is intended for readers 21 years of age or older.

Because the public site may be hosted by a third-party provider, that provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs in order to deliver the pages. We do not control a host’s logs, and their own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Cookies, ads, and similar technologies

The core pages of this site do not need cookies to function. If and when Google AdSense (or a later display-ad partner) is enabled, third parties will set cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and possibly to personalize ads based on your activity on this site and other sites.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s ad settings, and you may opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

We will not enable the AdSense script until a publisher account is approved and a publisher ID is added to the site. Until that happens, you should not see Google ad cookies from this site itself.

We do not currently embed a third-party analytics script. If that changes, this policy will be updated first to name the service and to describe what it collects.

Affiliate links and disclosures

Some articles may include affiliate links, including (if we later enroll) programs such as Amazon Associates or other retailer networks. If you click an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate links do not change the price you pay. A short disclosure also appears in the site header and footer on every page.

We do not sell your personal information as that phrase is commonly used in consumer privacy laws, and we do not operate a data-broker business.

Third-party sites

Articles may link to retailers, roasters, distilleries, government pages, or other websites. Those sites have their own policies and practices. Once you leave Cask & Cup, this policy no longer applies.

Alcohol-related content

Where this site discusses whiskey, bourbon, or other alcohol, the intended audience is adults 21 years of age or older in the United States. Nothing on the site is an offer to sell alcohol.

Data security and retention

Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation, unless a longer period is required by law. We are a small publisher and cannot promise that any transmission over the internet is perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can stop using the site at any time. You can control many cookies through your browser settings, and you can use the advertising opt-out links above. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, use the Contact page and say so.

Residents of places with specific privacy laws (including, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws, or the GDPR if you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom) may have additional rights to request access to or deletion of personal information we actually hold, such as an email you sent. Use the contact address on this site to make that request. We will need enough information to locate the message and to reasonably confirm the request.
